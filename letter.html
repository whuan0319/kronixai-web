A note from the founder

Why I built KronixAI

Hi — I’m Wesley, 33, and an athlete my whole life: tennis, volleyball, swimming, the gym six days a week. I’m super competitive and always want to perform at the highest level I could.

So when my sleep suddenly fell apart in the winter of 2023 — several awakenings throughout the night and feeling tired and not myself all day — I told myself it was a phase. It wasn’t. For 1.5 years I watched my body change: thinning hair, eyebags, injuries I’d never had before. My friends never noticed. I did. Looking back, it was just my body telling me it wasn’t able to cut the same corners anymore. I was simply just aging. The scary part about aging is that symptoms often hit randomly. An anomaly suddenly becomes the ordinary.

In the summer of 2025, I decided enough was enough. Using AI, I taught myself how to actually fix my sleep, eat, train, and play sustainably — what would’ve taken years, I picked up in months.

I built KronixAI so you don’t have to go through what I did: everything I learned, in one app that actually knows you, so you can become the best version of yourself.

Thanks for being here. I really hope you stick with it. It’ll be worth it. Trust me.

— Wes
